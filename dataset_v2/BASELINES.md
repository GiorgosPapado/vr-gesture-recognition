## Effect of the (root) pseudo-session

The 24 loose root files (one per class) form the pseudo-session `(root)` and the split puts that whole session in the TEST split. They are single examples recorded outside any session, so they may be a different distribution from the session recordings. Test metrics are therefore given with and without them. The npz used here contains 22 of them; rows of `splits.csv` that are not in the npz: Teleportation.json, Zoom.json.
